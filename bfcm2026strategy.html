07 · What we take into 2026Six lessons

What last year tells us to do differently

1

The money is made 27 Nov to 11 Dec

Last year that window produced 36% of the season's purchases on 23% of the spend, while budget stayed flat. This year we give it more of the budget, without adding to the total.

2

Christmas creative goes live two weeks earlier

Last year's winners started 20 to 25 Nov and still had to learn on the run. This year they go live by 9 to 13 Nov, so they're proven and fully spending when Black Friday arrives.

3

Lead with "it arrives, and it's on us"

The shipping message was the top seller in every phase. With the 11 Dec free shipping cut-off, an order-by message becomes the single most useful line in the account.

4

Keep Black Friday small

A gift with purchase on one day was enough. No discount, no margin hit, and the weekend still delivered.

5

Capacity sets the ceiling

Last year you were at full Christmas capacity in early November, which is why we didn't push. This year we plan the peak budget to the number of orders you can ship a day.

6

Social, email and ads together

December worked best when posting, email and the 12 Days of Christmas ran alongside the ads. Those posts cost $0.04 to $0.08 per engagement and drew 223 entries. Worth repeating.

Steps, not jumps

Every increase is 20% or less with a hold of about a week ($440 to $528 to $600), so Meta keeps its learning. Cuts after the cut-offs are immediate.

Capacity first

$600 a day only goes live if you can fulfil it. If peak capacity is lower, we cap the window there and bank the difference.

Inside each day

12 · Black Friday offerFor you to choose today

Bring Free Roasties Friday back, and give your list first go

Recommended Free Roasties Friday, again

Same as last year: a free pack of roast potatoes with every order, Friday 27 Nov only. People remember it, it costs little, and it worked. Email subscribers get it from Thursday evening.

Option B · gift on $150+ orders

Same gift, only on orders over the free shipping line. Lower cost per order, and it nudges small baskets over $150. Today's Meta basket is already $168, so most orders would qualify anyway.

Option C · a different gift

Yorkshire puddings or another Christmas-table item, if roasties are short. Whatever we pick needs deep stock for a 40-order day.

  • Teaser from Fri 20 Nov, with posts on your channels first and the best one used as the ad.
  • Offer ads scheduled to switch on and off by themselves, as last year.
  • No sitewide discount. Last year proved we don't need it.

15 · Google AdsGuidance for your team over the season

Google catches the demand Meta creates. Keep it ready for the peak

Meta builds the Christmas demand; a lot of it then searches "Parker's" or a product by name. These are the moves for your team running Google day to day.

Keep Brand fully funded

From 20 Nov to 22 Dec, check daily that Brand isn't limited by budget. It's the cheapest sale you'll get, and Meta's peak lands here.

Step Shopping with the peak

One 20% step up around 20 Nov, hold through 11 Dec if return holds on Shopify, then step back on the 12th. Same rhythm as Meta.

Seasonality adjustment, Black Friday only

In Google Ads, set a short adjustment for Fri 27 to Mon 30 Nov so bidding expects the higher conversion rate. Keep it to the event, never longer than a week.

Free Roasties as a Merchant Center promotion

Set it up as a free gift promotion so Shopping ads show the offer. Submit by 20 Nov; promotions need review time.

Dates in the ads

Promotion and sitelink assets for "Free delivery, order by 11 Dec", then "Express until 22 Dec". Give each an end date so nothing goes stale.

Gifts and stock

A gift card sitelink from 18 Dec. Keep the Shopify feed syncing daily so sold-out items drop out of Shopping on their own.

Two don'ts for the peak: no new campaigns or bid strategy changes between 20 Nov and 22 Dec, and judge Google on Shopify orders rather than the platform's own conversion count.
