New customers05

How we keep first-time buyers coming in

Exclude people who've already bought

We load the 6,665 people who've bought from you in the last six months and keep the main audiences away from them. Historically about 12% of Meta spend reached existing customers, and inside a smaller budget that money goes to new people instead.

Measure on your Shopify new customers

Weekly, alongside the combined cost per new customer line in the Friday update. Last week was 191 new customers at $25.62 each. That's the number this plan is built to protect.

Keep the new creative coming

Bonfire, Fish and Chips and Steak & Ale go into the existing audiences within the new budgets. Fresh creative is what keeps Meta finding new people.

Keep Brits Gone USA live

Creator ads reach people who've never seen us, and this one builds into the Christmas giveaway and the audience we'll sell to in December.

What to expect06

A smaller account, run tighter

Fewer orders, but cheaper ones go first

At each audience's average cost, the saving is worth up to about 30 Meta purchases a week, against 119 last week. Likely fewer, because the last dollars in each audience are the least efficient ones.

Reported return may dip a little

Repeat buyers are the cheapest orders Meta finds, so excluding them lowers the headline figure. It's the right trade when new customers are the goal.

Room to go further if needed

After 5 to 7 days we can take another 20% off the three main audiences, to $393 a day. That saves $1,820 a week, done in two steps so nothing resets.

Checked every day

If new customers fall by more than 15% week on week, or cost per purchase sits above $45 for three days, we rebalance straight away.

Christmas07

Set up now for the biggest month of the year

Last December was the strongest month on record: 887 purchases at $10.29 each. Black Friday falls on 27 November this year, and Meta needs a week or two to ramp back up. Agreeing now when budget returns, ideally from early November, means we arrive at the season warm rather than starting from cold.

Google stays as it is at about $77 a day, and Shopping is reaching more of its auctions each week as the feed work lands.
