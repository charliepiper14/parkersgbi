What is genuinely working

  • Meta creative is performing better than June. Link clicks up 11.0% and add-to-cart rate up from 42.35% to 47.64%. The ads are creating more intent per dollar.

How To Read This Deck

Three numbers, measured three different ways

Meta and Google count differently and both overlap with the store. Every figure in this deck has been validated in Python against the raw platform exports at account, campaign and ad set level. Where the two platforms disagree with Shopify, we treat that as an attribution difference until it is reconciled, not as a performance finding.

Meta

Counts purchases

Meta reported 586 purchases worth $90,848.51 on a 7 day click and 1 day view window. This is the closest platform number to a real order.

Google

Counts conversions

Google counted 658.45 conversions worth $45,271.89. Conversions include secondary actions and arrive as fractions, so the count runs high against real orders.

Shopify

Counts reality

The store remains the single source of truth for revenue and for real new customers. Every efficiency claim here is written to be reconciled back to it.

The rule we hold to

We never add Meta and Google figures together and we never compare their returns side by side. Both platforms claim some of the same orders and they count in different ways. Where a single efficiency number is needed, it is total paid spend against real Shopify new customers.

One reporting note. The Google figure reported to you on 31 July was $42,006 of conversion value on $2,983 of spend. The closed month is $45,271.89 on $3,082.05. The difference is late attribution filling in on the final days plus the last day of spend, which is normal and expected. June closed at $56,053.28 and is unchanged from what was reported at the time.

A Shopify checkout funnel export for June against July, cart to checkout to completed order. If Shopify shows the same shape, this is the account's number one priority for August and it sits with the store, starting with the $150 free shipping threshold against a $155.03 average basket.
